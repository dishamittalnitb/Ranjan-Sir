## Common Question Patterns by Chapter

### Ch15 (Chemical Reactions)
- Balancing equations
- Corrosion/Rusting (conditions, prevention)
- Electrolysis of water (mass ratio H:O = 1:8)
- Rancidity (oxidation of fats/oils)
- Activity-based lab observations

### Ch16 (Acids, Bases, Salts)
- pH scale (identify from values)
- Neutralisation reactions
- Salt family: NaCl, NaHCO3 (baking soda), Na2CO3, Bleaching powder (CaOCl2)
- POP (Plaster of Paris) and gypsum (CaSO4.2H2O → CaSO4.½H2O)
- Water of crystallisation

### Ch25 (Electricity)
- Ohm's Law numericals
- Series/Parallel resistance calculations
- Electric power (P = VI = I²R = V²/R)
- Joule's heating law
- Domestic circuits (earth wire, fuse, short circuit)
- Alloys as heating elements (nichrome)

### Ch23 (Light)
- Mirror formula (1/f = 1/v + 1/u) and lens formula
- Magnification (m = -v/u for mirrors, v/u for lenses)
- Ray diagrams for concave/convex mirrors and lenses
- Sign conventions (Cartesian)
- Refractive index and Snell's law
