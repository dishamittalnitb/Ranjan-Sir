# Chapter 06: Lines and Angles (Class IX)

## Concept Summary

This chapter studies the fundamental relationships between lines and angles:

1. **Basic Definitions**: Line segment, ray, collinear points, intersecting and non-intersecting lines. Types of angles: acute, obtuse, right, straight, reflex, complementary, supplementary, adjacent, vertically opposite, linear pair.

2. **Linear Pair Axiom** (stated without proof): If a ray stands on a line, the sum of the two adjacent angles so formed is 180 degrees, and the converse.

3. **Vertically Opposite Angles** (provable): If two lines intersect, vertically opposite angles are equal.

4. **Parallel Lines**: Lines that do not intersect. A transversal is a line that intersects two or more lines at distinct points.

5. **Transversal Properties** (stated without proof): When a transversal intersects two parallel lines:
   - Corresponding angles are equal
   - Alternate interior angles are equal
   - Interior angles on the same side of the transversal are supplementary (sum = 180 degrees)

6. **Parallel Line Criterion**: If any of the above angle relations holds, the lines are parallel.

7. **Lines Parallel to a Given Line** (stated without proof): Lines which are parallel to a given line are parallel to each other.

8. **Angle Sum Property of a Triangle** (provable): The sum of the three angles of a triangle is 180 degrees.

9. **Exterior Angle Property** (stated without proof): If a side of a triangle is produced, the exterior angle so formed equals the sum of the two interior opposite angles.

## Key Formulas/Theorems

**Linear Pair**: Angle1 + Angle2 = 180 degrees (when they form a linear pair)

**Vertically Opposite Angles**: Angle AOD = Angle BOC (when AB and CD intersect at O)

**Angle Sum of Triangle**: In triangle ABC, Angle A + Angle B + Angle C = 180 degrees

**Exterior Angle**: Exterior Angle ACD = Angle A + Angle B (if BC is extended to D)

**Parallel Line Angle Relations**: When a transversal cuts parallel lines:
- Angle of corresponding position: equal
- Alternate interior: equal
- Co-interior (same side interior): sum = 180 degrees
