# Chapter 20: Iswaran the Storyteller

## Theme & Summary

**Theme:** The story explores the power of storytelling and imagination. Through the character of Iswaran, it shows how a skilled narrator can make the ordinary extraordinary and blur the lines between reality and fiction. It also contains elements of suspense and horror.

**Summary:** The narrator, Mahendra, is a junior supervisor who moves from place to place for his work. His cook and attendant, Iswaran, accompanies him everywhere. Iswaran is a master storyteller who entertains Mahendra daily with dramatic tales — from taming a mad elephant on the street to supernatural encounters. One such story is about a female ghost holding a foetus in her arms who haunts the factory area where they live. Mahendra dismisses it as fiction but becomes increasingly uneasy. One full moon night, Mahendra himself sees a dark, ghostly figure outside his window. Terrified, he resolves to leave the place immediately, and the story ends with Iswaran's satisfied comment: "Sir, you are a very educated man. Do you think you could be seeing ghosts?"

## Character List

- **Iswaran:** Mahendra's cook; an extraordinary storyteller who narrates thrilling and suspenseful tales with great flair and dramatic gestures.
- **Mahendra:** The narrator; a junior supervisor who listens to Iswaran's stories but is eventually unsettled by the ghost story.

## Key Quotes

1. "Iswaran's stories were always so vivid that they seemed real."
2. "The elephant went berserk, broke into a market, and created havoc."
3. "I saw a figure, dark and ghostly, carrying a bundle."
4. "Do you think you could be seeing ghosts, sir?"
