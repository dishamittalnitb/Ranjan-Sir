# Chapter 28: Water Resources

## Learning Outcomes
The students will be able to:
- Examine the reasons for conservation of water resource in India.
- Analyse and infer how the multipurpose projects are supporting the requirement of water.

## Concept Summary / Key Points
1. **Water Scarcity**: Despite being a renewable resource, water scarcity affects many regions. India receives about 4% of the world's precipitation but is home to about 17.5% of the world's population.
2. **Causes of Water Scarcity**:
   - Uneven distribution of rainfall across regions and seasons.
   - Over-exploitation of groundwater for irrigation (Green Revolution areas like Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan).
   - Population growth, urbanization, and industrialization.
   - Water pollution from industrial, agricultural, and domestic waste.
   - Poor water management and wastage.
3. **Multi-Purpose River Projects**:
   - Dams are built for irrigation, hydroelectric power generation, flood control, water supply, inland navigation, and fisheries.
   - Jawaharlal Nehru called dams "Temples of Modern India."
   - **Key Dams/Projects**: Bhakra Nangal (Sutlej), Hirakud (Mahanadi), Nagarjuna Sagar (Krishna), Sardar Sarovar (Narmada), Tehri (Bhagirathi), Rana Pratap Sagar (Chambal).
4. **Criticism of Multi-Purpose Projects**:
   - Large-scale displacement of local people, often tribals and farmers.
   - Submergence of forests and fertile agricultural land.
   - Ecological consequences: sedimentation, waterlogging, soil salinity, and damage to aquatic life.
   - Narmada Bachao Andolan: Movement against the Sardar Sarovar Project, led by Medha Patkar.
5. **Rainwater Harvesting**: An alternative and sustainable method of water conservation. Methods include:
   - **Rooftop harvesting**: Collecting rainwater from rooftops into storage tanks (common in Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu).
   - **Khadins and Johads**: Traditional water harvesting structures in Rajasthan.
   - **Kuls and Guls**: Traditional irrigation channels in hill and mountainous regions (Himachal Pradesh).
   - Bamboo drip irrigation in Meghalaya.
   - Tamil Nadu has made rooftop rainwater harvesting mandatory for all houses.

## Key Terms and Definitions
- **Water Scarcity**: The lack of sufficient available water resources to meet water demands.
- **Multi-Purpose River Project**: A dam-based project that serves multiple purposes: irrigation, power, flood control, etc.
- **Dam**: A barrier constructed across a river to store water.
- **Rainwater Harvesting**: The collection and storage of rainwater for reuse.
- **Groundwater**: Water located beneath the earth's surface in soil pore spaces and rock fractures.
- **Waterlogging**: Saturation of land with water, often caused by over-irrigation.
- **Narmada Bachao Andolan (NBA)**: A social movement against the construction of large dams on the Narmada River.
- **Bhakra Nangal Project**: A major multipurpose project on the Sutlej River.
- **Sardar Sarovar Project**: A multipurpose dam on the Narmada River.
