# Chapter 09: Force and Newton's Laws

## 1. Concept Summary

### 1.1 Force
- A push or pull that tends to change the state of rest or motion, or the shape/size of an object.
- **SI unit:** Newton (N). 1 N = 1 kg m/s^2
- **CGS unit:** dyne. 1 N = 10^5 dyne.
- **Types:** Balanced forces (net force = 0, no change in state) and Unbalanced forces (net force not equal to 0, causes acceleration).

### 1.2 Newton's First Law of Motion (Law of Inertia)
- An object remains in a state of rest or of uniform motion in a straight line unless compelled to change that state by an external force.
- Defines **inertia**: The tendency of a body to resist a change in its state of rest or motion.
- **Inertia of rest:** Tendency to remain at rest (e.g., passenger falls backward when bus starts).
- **Inertia of motion:** Tendency to remain in motion (e.g., passenger falls forward when bus stops).
- **Inertia of direction:** Tendency to continue in the same direction (e.g., stone leaves tangentially from rotating string).

### 1.3 Mass and Inertia
- Mass is a measure of inertia. Greater the mass, greater the inertia.
- Heavier objects are harder to start/stop than lighter objects.

### 1.4 Newton's Second Law of Motion
- The rate of change of momentum of a body is directly proportional to the applied force and takes place in the direction of the force.
- **Force (F) = mass (m) x acceleration (a)**, i.e., F = ma
- Defines momentum (p = mv): Product of mass and velocity; SI unit: kg m/s.

### 1.5 Momentum
- p = mv (mass x velocity)
- Vector quantity; direction same as velocity.
- Larger the mass or velocity, larger the momentum.

### 1.6 Newton's Third Law of Motion
- To every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.
- Action and reaction act on two different bodies.
- Examples: Recoil of a gun, walking (pushing ground backward, ground pushes forward), rocket propulsion, a boatman pushing water backward to move forward.

### 1.7 Conservation of Momentum
- **Note from syllabus:** This topic is included but assessed only formatively. It is not assessed in summative/board examinations.
- In the absence of an external force, the total momentum of a system remains conserved.
- m1u1 + m2u2 = m1v1 + m2v2
- Applications: Recoil of gun (gun + bullet system), collision of two objects.
