# English Grammar Reference (Classes IX & X)

## Overview

Grammar is assessed in **Section B (Writing Skills and Grammar, 10 marks)** for both Classes IX and X. The grammar section tests accurate use of spelling, punctuation, and grammatical structures through Gap Filling, Editing, and Transformation exercises. Students must attempt **ten out of twelve questions**.

## Grammar Topics (Common to Both Classes)

The CBSE syllabus specifies the following grammar topics to be reinforced at the secondary level:

### 1. Determiners
- Articles (a, an, the)
- Demonstratives (this, that, these, those)
- Possessives (my, your, his, her, its, our, their)
- Quantifiers (some, any, many, much, few, little, several, all, both, each, every, either, neither)
- Numbers (one, first, etc.)

**Key Exam Focus:** Gap-filling exercises with correct determiner; editing errors in determiner usage.

### 2. Tenses
- Present: Simple, Continuous, Perfect, Perfect Continuous
- Past: Simple, Continuous, Perfect, Perfect Continuous
- Future: Simple, Continuous, Perfect, Perfect Continuous
- Sequence of tenses in complex sentences

**Key Exam Focus:** Correct verb forms in context; tense consistency in paragraphs; transformation exercises.

### 3. Modals (Modal Auxiliaries)
- **Ability:** can, could
- **Permission:** may, can, could, might
- **Obligation/Necessity:** must, have to, need to, should, ought to
- **Possibility/Probability:** may, might, could, must
- **Advice:** should, ought to, had better
- **Past Modals:** could have, should have, must have, might have

**Key Exam Focus:** Choosing the appropriate modal for a given context; transformation from direct to indirect with modal changes.

### 4. Subject-Verb Concord
- Singular subject takes singular verb; plural subject takes plural verb
- Collective nouns (team, family, jury) — usually singular verb
- Either/or, neither/nor — verb agrees with the nearer subject
- Each, every, one — always singular verb
- Nouns plural in form but singular in meaning (news, mathematics)
- Words joined by "and" — usually plural verb
- Phrases like "along with," "together with," "as well as" — verb agrees with the first subject

**Key Exam Focus:** Error correction in subject-verb agreement; gap-filling with correct verb forms.

### 5. Reported Speech
- Commands and Requests: Change imperative to infinitive (said to -> ordered/requested/commanded + to + verb)
- Statements: Change tense (present to past), pronouns, time/place expressions
- Questions: Change word order from interrogative to statement; "if/whether" for yes/no questions; no conjunction for wh- questions

**Key Transformation Rules:**
| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---|---|
| this | that |
| these | those |
| here | there |
| now | then |
| today | that day |
| tomorrow | the next/following day |
| yesterday | the previous day / the day before |
| ago | before |
| will | would |
| can | could |
| may | might |
| must | had to / must |

**Key Exam Focus:** Transformation exercises; editing indirect speech errors.

### 6. Additional Language Items (Secondary Level)
- Non-finites (infinitives, gerunds, participles)
- Conditional clauses (if, unless, provided that)
- Complex and compound sentences (coordination and subordination)
- Phrasal verbs and prepositional phrases
- Cohesive devices (linking words, discourse markers)
- Punctuation (semicolon, colon, dash, hyphen, parenthesis/brackets, exclamation mark)

## Exam Pattern for Grammar (10 Marks)

- **Question Type:** Gap Filling / Editing / Transformation exercises
- **Number of Questions:** 12 questions given; 10 must be attempted
- **Marking:** 1 mark each

## Study Strategy

1. Master the core five topics (Determiners, Tenses, Modals, Subject-Verb Concord, Reported Speech) as these are explicitly listed.
2. Practice gap-filling and editing exercises daily — these test application, not just theory.
3. Focus on tense consistency within paragraphs and reported speech transformations.
4. Use the Words and Expressions workbook for targeted grammar practice.
5. Review punctuation rules for editing exercises.

## Internal Assessment

Grammar accuracy is also assessed as part of the Listening and Speaking Skills (ALS, 5 marks) under the "Language (grammar and vocabulary)" parameter of the ALS rubric.
