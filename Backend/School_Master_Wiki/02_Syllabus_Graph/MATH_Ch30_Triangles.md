# Chapter 30: Triangles (Class X)

## Concept Summary

This chapter studies similarity of triangles:

1. **Similar Figures**: Two figures with the same shape but not necessarily the same size. Examples and counter-examples.

2. **Similar Triangles**: Triangles whose corresponding angles are equal and corresponding sides are proportional. Notation: triangle ABC ~ triangle PQR.

3. **Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT)** (provable): If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle intersecting the other two sides at distinct points, the other two sides are divided in the same ratio. Also known as Thales Theorem.

4. **Converse of BPT** (stated without proof): If a line divides any two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, then the line is parallel to the third side.

5. **Similarity Criteria**:
   - **AAA Criterion** (stated without proof): If corresponding angles of two triangles are equal, they are similar.
   - **SSS Criterion** (stated without proof): If corresponding sides are proportional, triangles are similar.
   - **SAS Criterion** (stated without proof): If one angle of a triangle equals one angle of another triangle and sides including these angles are proportional, the triangles are similar.

6. **Applications**: Proving similarity, finding unknown lengths, proving geometric theorems.

## Key Formulas/Theorems

**Basic Proportionality Theorem**: In triangle ABC, if DE || BC (D on AB, E on AC):
AD/DB = AE/EC

**Similarity Criteria**: AAA, SSS, SAS

**Area Ratio Theorem**: If triangle ABC ~ triangle PQR:
Area(ABC) / Area(PQR) = (AB/PQ)^2 = (BC/QR)^2 = (CA/RP)^2

**Pythagoras Theorem** (provable via similarity): In a right triangle, the square of the hypotenuse equals the sum of squares of the other two sides.

**Converse of Pythagoras**: If in a triangle, the square of one side equals the sum of squares of the other two sides, the angle opposite the first side is a right angle.
