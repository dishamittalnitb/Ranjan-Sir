# Chapter 07: Triangles (Class IX)

## Concept Summary

This chapter develops the theory of congruence of triangles:

1. **Congruence**: Two figures are congruent if they have the same shape and size. For triangles, this means all corresponding sides and angles are equal.

2. **SAS Congruence Rule** (stated without proof): If two sides and the included angle of one triangle are equal (respectively) to two sides and the included angle of the other triangle, the triangles are congruent.

3. **ASA Congruence Rule** (provable): If two angles and the included side of one triangle are equal (respectively) to two angles and the included side of the other triangle, the triangles are congruent.

4. **SSS Congruence Rule** (stated without proof): If three sides of one triangle are equal (respectively) to three sides of the other triangle, the triangles are congruent.

5. **RHS Congruence Rule** (stated without proof): If the hypotenuse and one side of a right triangle are equal to the hypotenuse and one side of another right triangle, the triangles are congruent.

6. **Properties of Isosceles Triangles**:
   - (Provable) Angles opposite to equal sides of a triangle are equal.
   - (Stated without proof) Sides opposite to equal angles of a triangle are equal.

## Key Formulas/Theorems

**Congruence Criteria**:
- **SAS**: Side-Angle-Side
- **ASA**: Angle-Side-Angle
- **SSS**: Side-Side-Side
- **RHS**: Right angle-Hypotenuse-Side

**Isosceles Triangle Properties**:
- If AB = AC, then Angle B = Angle C
- If Angle B = Angle C, then AB = AC

**Some Important Results Derivable from Congruence**:
- Perpendicular bisector of a line segment is the locus of points equidistant from the endpoints.
- In a triangle, equal sides produce equal angles opposite them.
