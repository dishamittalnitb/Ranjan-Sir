# Chapter 11: Surface Areas and Volumes (Class IX)

## Concept Summary

This chapter extends mensuration to three-dimensional solids -- spheres, hemispheres, and right circular cones:

1. **Sphere**: A perfectly round geometrical object in three-dimensional space. All points on the surface are equidistant from the center.

2. **Hemisphere**: Half of a sphere, divided by a plane passing through the center.

3. **Right Circular Cone**: A solid generated by rotating a right-angled triangle about one of its perpendicular sides.

4. **Surface Areas**: Curved Surface Area (CSA) and Total Surface Area (TSA) for each solid.

5. **Volumes**: Space occupied by the solid.

## Key Formulas/Theorems

**Sphere** (radius = r):
- Curved Surface Area (CSA) = 4 * pi * r^2
- Total Surface Area (TSA) = 4 * pi * r^2 (same as CSA)
- Volume = (4/3) * pi * r^3

**Hemisphere** (radius = r):
- Curved Surface Area (CSA) = 2 * pi * r^2
- Total Surface Area (TSA) = 3 * pi * r^2
- Volume = (2/3) * pi * r^3

**Right Circular Cone** (radius = r, slant height = l, height = h):
- Slant height: l = sqrt(r^2 + h^2)
- Curved Surface Area (CSA) = pi * r * l
- Total Surface Area (TSA) = pi * r * (l + r)
- Volume = (1/3) * pi * r^2 * h
