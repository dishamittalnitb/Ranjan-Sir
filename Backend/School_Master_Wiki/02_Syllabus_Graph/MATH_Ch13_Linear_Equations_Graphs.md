# Chapter 13: Linear Equations Graphs [FORMATIVE ONLY]

## Concept Summary

This formative-only topic extends the concept of linear equations to their graphical representation:

1. **Graph of Linear Equation**: Every linear equation ax + by + c = 0 represents a straight line in the Cartesian plane. Plotting involves finding ordered pair solutions and joining them.

2. **Plotting Method**: Find at least two solutions as ordered pairs, plot them, and draw the line through them. For accuracy, three points are recommended (the third as verification).

3. **Real-Life Applications**: Problems from real life involving ratio and proportion, where algebraic and graphical solutions are done simultaneously. Examples include:
   - Work and time problems
   - Cost-quantity relationships
   - Distance-time-speed problems
   - Mixture problems

4. **Graphical Analysis**: Interpretation of graphs to understand relationships between variables, intersection points, and trends.

## Key Formulas/Theorems

**Equation Form**: ax + by + c = 0

**Finding Solutions**: 
- For x = 0: y = -c/b
- For y = 0: x = -c/a
- Alternatively, choose any x, compute corresponding y

**Plotting**: Points (x, y) in the Cartesian plane
