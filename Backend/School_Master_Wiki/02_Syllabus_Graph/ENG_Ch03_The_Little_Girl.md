# Chapter 03: The Little Girl

## Theme & Summary

**Theme:** The story explores the complex emotional relationship between a young girl and her father, highlighting how children often misinterpret parental strictness as a lack of love. It emphasizes empathy, understanding, and the deep but sometimes hidden affection within families.

**Summary:** Kezia is a little girl who is terribly afraid of her strict, demanding father, whom she perceives as a giant figure who is always scolding. She compares him unfavorably to her kind grandmother and friendly neighbor Mr. Macdonald. One day, after a nightmare, she finds comfort in her mother's bed; when her father returns from work, she is punished. However, when her mother is hospitalized and her grandmother is away, Kezia is left alone with her father. After a nightmare, her father takes her to his bed, and for the first time, she feels his warmth and love, realizing he is not so frightening after all and that he too has a big heart.

## Character List

- **Kezia:** The protagonist; a sensitive little girl who initially fears her father but eventually understands his love.
- **Kezia's Father:** A busy, strict man who appears intimidating but loves his daughter deeply; works hard for the family.
- **Kezia's Mother:** Often ill and hospitalized; a background character.
- **Grandmother:** Kezia's confidante who encourages her to connect with her father.
- **Mr. Macdonald:** The friendly neighbor who plays joyfully with his children, providing a contrast to Kezia's father.

## Key Quotes

1. "What did God make fathers for?" - Kezia's innocent question reflecting her fear.
2. "He was a figure to be feared and avoided."
3. "She crept into his bed and he half woke, and put his arm across her."
4. "She realised that her father was not so bad after all. He had a big heart."
