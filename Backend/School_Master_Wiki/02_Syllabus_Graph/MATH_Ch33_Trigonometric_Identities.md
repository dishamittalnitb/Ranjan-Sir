# Chapter 33: Trigonometric Identities (Class X)

## Concept Summary

This chapter develops and applies fundamental trigonometric identities:

1. **Fundamental Identity** (provable): sin^2(A) + cos^2(A) = 1 for all values of A.
   - Proof uses the Pythagorean theorem on a right triangle.

2. **Derived Identities** (from sin^2(A) + cos^2(A) = 1, dividing by cos^2(A) or sin^2(A)):
   - 1 + tan^2(A) = sec^2(A)
   - 1 + cot^2(A) = cosec^2(A)

3. **Applications**: Proving simple trigonometric identities and solving problems. Only simple identities are required (not highly complex ones).

4. **Verification**: Using the fundamental identities to simplify and verify trigonometric expressions.

## Key Formulas/Theorems

**Primary Identity**: sin^2(A) + cos^2(A) = 1

**Derived Identities**:
- 1 + tan^2(A) = sec^2(A)
- 1 + cot^2(A) = cosec^2(A)

**Useful Manipulations**:
- sin^2(A) = 1 - cos^2(A)
- cos^2(A) = 1 - sin^2(A)
- sec^2(A) - tan^2(A) = 1
- cosec^2(A) - cot^2(A) = 1

**Tips for Proving Identities**:
1. Start with the more complex side
2. Express everything in terms of sin and cos
3. Look for opportunities to apply sin^2(A) + cos^2(A) = 1
4. Simplify step by step
