# Chapter 23: Light - Reflection and Refraction

## 1. Concept Summary

### 1.1 Reflection of Light
The bouncing back of light when it strikes a polished surface. Laws of reflection:
1. Angle of incidence (i) = Angle of reflection (r).
2. The incident ray, the reflected ray, and the normal to the surface all lie in the same plane.

### 1.2 Spherical Mirrors
A spherical mirror is a mirror whose reflecting surface is a part of a hollow sphere.

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Pole (P)** | Centre of the mirror surface. |
| **Centre of Curvature (C)** | Centre of the sphere of which the mirror is a part. |
| **Radius of Curvature (R)** | Distance PC (radius of the sphere). |
| **Principal Axis** | The straight line joining C and P. |
| **Principal Focus (F)** | Point on the principal axis where parallel rays converge (concave) or appear to diverge from (convex). |
| **Focal Length (f)** | Distance PF. f = R/2. |

#### Types of Spherical Mirrors
| Mirror | Nature | Image Formation |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| **Concave (Converging)** | Reflecting surface curves inward | Real or virtual depending on object position |
| **Convex (Diverging)** | Reflecting surface bulges outward | Always virtual, erect, diminished |

### 1.3 Image Formation by Concave Mirror
| Object Position | Image Position | Size | Nature |
|-----------------|---------------|------|--------|
| At infinity | At F | Highly diminished (point size) | Real, inverted |
| Beyond C | Between F and C | Diminished | Real, inverted |
| At C | At C | Same size | Real, inverted |
| Between C and F | Beyond C | Enlarged | Real, inverted |
| At F | At infinity | Highly enlarged | Real, inverted |
| Between P and F | Behind the mirror | Enlarged | Virtual, erect |

### 1.4 Image Formation by Convex Mirror
- Object anywhere in front -- Always virtual, erect, diminished, behind the mirror between P and F.

### 1.5 Mirror Formula and Magnification
- **Mirror formula:** 1/f = 1/v + 1/u
  - f = focal length (negative for concave, positive for convex - as per Cartesian sign convention)
  - u = object distance (always negative)
  - v = image distance
- **Magnification (m):** Ratio of height of image (h') to height of object (h).
  - m = h'/h = -v/u
  - Positive m = Virtual/Erect; Negative m = Real/Inverted.
  - |m| > 1 = Enlarged; |m| = 1 = Same size; |m| < 1 = Diminished.

### 1.6 Refraction of Light
The bending of light when it passes from one medium to another. Cause: Change in speed of light in different media.
- **Laws of Refraction (Snell's Law):**
  1. The incident ray, the refracted ray, and the normal all lie in the same plane.
  2. n1 sin i = n2 sin r (n = refractive index).

### 1.7 Refractive Index
- The ratio of the speed of light in vacuum (c) to the speed of light in the medium (v): **n = c/v**
- Absolute refractive index: n = c/v (always > 1).
- Relative refractive index (medium 2 w.r.t medium 1): n21 = n2/n1 = v1/v2.
- Higher refractive index means slower speed of light (optically denser medium).

### 1.8 Spherical Lenses
A lens is a transparent medium bounded by two spherical surfaces (or one plane, one spherical).

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Optical Centre (O)** | Centre point of the lens. |
| **Principal Focus (F)** | Point where parallel rays converge (convex) or appear to diverge from (concave). |
| **Focal Length (f)** | Distance OF. |

#### Types of Lenses
| Lens | Nature | Image Formation |
|------|--------|----------------|
| **Convex (Converging)** | Thicker in the centre, thinner at edges | Real or virtual depending on object position |
| **Concave (Diverging)** | Thinner in the centre, thicker at edges | Always virtual, erect, diminished |

### 1.9 Image Formation by Convex Lens
| Object Position | Image Position | Size | Nature |
|-----------------|---------------|------|--------|
| At infinity | At F (other side) | Highly diminished (point) | Real, inverted |
| Beyond 2F | Between F and 2F (other side) | Diminished | Real, inverted |
| At 2F | At 2F (other side) | Same size | Real, inverted |
| Between F and 2F | Beyond 2F (other side) | Enlarged | Real, inverted |
| At F | At infinity | Highly enlarged | Real, inverted |
| Between O and F | Same side of lens | Enlarged | Virtual, erect |

### 1.10 Lens Formula and Magnification
- **Lens formula:** 1/f = 1/v - 1/u
- **Magnification:** m = h'/h = v/u

### 1.11 Power of a Lens
- **Power (P) = 1 / f (in metres)**
- SI unit: Dioptre (D). 1 D = 1 m^-1.
- Convex lens: Positive power; Concave lens: Negative power.
- For a combination of lenses: P = P1 + P2 + P3 + ...

### 1.12 Sign Convention (Cartesian/New Cartesian)
- All distances measured from Pole (mirror) or Optical Centre (lens).
- Distances measured in the direction of incident light: Positive.
- Distances measured opposite to the direction of incident light: Negative.
- Heights above principal axis: Positive; Below: Negative.

## 2. Key Formulas

| Formula | Application |
|---------|------------|
| **Mirror Formula** | 1/f = 1/v + 1/u |
| **Magnification (Mirror)** | m = -v/u = h'/h |
| **Lens Formula** | 1/f = 1/v - 1/u |
| **Magnification (Lens)** | m = v/u = h'/h |
| **Power of Lens** | P = 1/f (m) |
| **Focal Length & Radius** | f = R/2 (mirror) |
| **Refractive Index** | n = c/v (speed of light in vacuum / speed in medium) |
| **Snell's Law** | n1 sin i = n2 sin r |
