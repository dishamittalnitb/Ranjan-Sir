# Chapter 06: India - Size and Location

## Learning Outcomes
The students will be able to:
- Examine how the location of an area impacts its climate and time with reference to longitude and latitude.
- Explore and analyse the trading and cultural relationships of India with its neighbouring countries.
- Evaluate the situation and reasons that made 82.5degE longitude the Time Meridian of India.
- Examine how the location of India enables its position as a strategic partner in the subcontinent.
- Justify the reasons for the differences in climatic conditions, local and standard time.

## Concept Summary / Key Points
1. **Location of India**: India lies entirely in the Northern Hemisphere. The mainland extends between latitudes 8deg4'N and 37deg6'N and longitudes 68deg7'E and 97deg25'E.
2. **Size of India**: Total area of approximately 3.28 million sq km (seventh-largest country). India has a land boundary of about 15,200 km and a coastline of about 7,516.6 km.
3. **Tropic of Cancer (23deg30'N)**: Passes through 8 states -- Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura, and Mizoram. Divides India into tropical and subtropical zones.
4. **Standard Meridian of India (82deg30'E)**: Passes through Mirzapur (UP). India's standard time is 5 hours 30 minutes ahead of GMT (Greenwich Mean Time).
5. **India's Strategic Location**: Centrally located between East and West Asia; trans-Indian Ocean routes connect European countries in the west to East Asian countries. India has the longest coastline on the Indian Ocean.
6. **Neighbouring Countries**: India shares land borders with Pakistan, Afghanistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Myanmar, and Bangladesh. Maritime neighbours include Sri Lanka and Maldives.
7. **States and Union Territories**: India comprises 28 States and 8 Union Territories.

## Key Terms and Definitions
- **Latitude**: The angular distance of a place north or south of the equator.
- **Longitude**: The angular distance of a place east or west of the Prime Meridian.
- **Standard Meridian**: The reference longitude for a country's uniform standard time.
- **IST (Indian Standard Time)**: The time adopted all over India, calculated at 82deg30'E longitude.
- **Tropic of Cancer**: The latitude line at 23deg30'N that marks the northern limit of the tropical zone.
- **Subcontinent**: A large, distinguishable part of a continent. India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan, Sri Lanka, and Maldives form the Indian subcontinent.
- **Coastline**: The boundary where land meets the ocean.
- **GMT (Greenwich Mean Time)**: The time at the Prime Meridian (0deg longitude), used as a reference for world time zones.
