# Chapter 12: Work, Energy and Power

## 1. Concept Summary

### 1.1 Work
- Work is done when a force acts on an object and the object moves in the direction of the force.
- **Work (W) = Force (F) x Displacement (s)** --> W = Fs (for force along direction of displacement).
- For force at an angle theta: **W = F s cos(theta)**
- Conditions for work: (i) A force must act, (ii) Displacement must occur in the direction of force.
- **SI unit:** Joule (J). 1 J = 1 N x 1 m. (Work done when 1 N force displaces an object by 1 m).
- **Zero work:** (i) Force is zero, (ii) Displacement is zero, (iii) Force and displacement are perpendicular (theta = 90 degree).
- Work can be positive (0 <= theta < 90 degree), negative (90 < theta <= 180 degree), or zero (theta = 90 degree).

### 1.2 Energy
- The capacity to do work.
- **SI unit:** Joule (J) - same as work.
- Major forms: Mechanical (KE + PE), Heat, Chemical, Electrical, Light, Sound, Nuclear.
- **Commercial unit of energy excluded from assessment syllabus** (kilowatt-hour not required for exam).

### 1.3 Kinetic Energy (KE)
- Energy possessed by a body due to its motion.
- **KE = (1/2) mv^2**
- Factors: mass (directly proportional) and square of velocity (directly proportional to v^2).
- Derivation: W = Fs = mas = m(v^2 - u^2)/2 = (1/2)mv^2 - (1/2)mu^2 (Work-Energy theorem).

### 1.4 Potential Energy (PE)
- Energy possessed by a body due to its position or configuration.
- **Gravitational PE = mgh** (m = mass, g = acceleration due to gravity, h = height from reference level).
- Work done to raise an object to height h against gravity = mgh, stored as PE.

### 1.5 Law of Conservation of Energy
- Energy can neither be created nor destroyed; it can only be transformed from one form to another.
- Total energy of an isolated system remains constant.
- **Examples:**
  - Freely falling body: PE decreases, KE increases, TE = constant (mgh_max = (1/2)mv^2_max).
  - Pendulum: PE at extremes converts to KE at mean position.
  - Stretching a rubber band: Muscular energy --> PE (elastic).
  - Photosynthesis: Light energy --> Chemical energy.

### 1.6 Power
- The rate of doing work or rate of transfer of energy.
- **Power (P) = Work / Time = W / t**
- **SI unit:** Watt (W). 1 W = 1 J/s.
- Average power = Total work done / Total time.

## 2. Key Formulas/Definitions

| Term | Formula |
|------|---------|
| **Work** | W = F s cos(theta) |
| **Kinetic Energy** | KE = (1/2) mv^2 |
| **Potential Energy** | PE = mgh |
| **Conservation of Energy** | KE + PE = constant (for isolated system) |
| **Power** | P = W / t |
| **Work-Energy Theorem** | W = Change in KE = (1/2)m(v^2 - u^2) |
| **1 Joule** | 1 N of force displacing an object by 1 m in direction of force |
| **1 Watt** | 1 J of work done in 1 second |

## 3. Board Exam Weightage & Question Pattern

Falls under **Unit III: Motion, Force and Work (27 marks)**.

- **VSA (1 mark):** Define work, energy, power; SI units; define KE and PE.
- **SA (2-3 marks):** Derive KE expression; explain conservation of energy with examples; differentiate KE and PE; power calculation; conditions for zero work.
- **LA (5 marks):** State law of conservation of energy and prove for freely falling body; numerical combining KE, PE, work, power.
- **Numerical Problems:** Work calculation; KE and PE problems; power calculations; conservation of energy numericals.
- **Note:** Commercial unit of energy (kWh) excluded from summative assessment.
