# Chapter 01: Real Numbers (Class IX)

## Concept Summary

This chapter deepens the understanding of the real number system. It covers:

1. **Review of Number Systems**: Natural numbers (N), integers (Z), rational numbers (Q) and their representation on the number line. Representation of terminating and non-terminating recurring decimals through successive magnification.

2. **Rational Numbers as Decimals**: Rational numbers can be expressed as terminating or non-terminating recurring decimals, and conversely, every such decimal represents a rational number.

3. **Irrational Numbers**: Existence of non-rational (irrational) numbers such as sqrt(2), sqrt(3). These are non-terminating, non-recurring decimals. Every real number is represented by a unique point on the number line and vice versa.

4. **Definition of nth root**: Definition of nth root of a real number.

5. **Rationalization**: Rationalizing denominators of the form `1/(a + b*sqrt(x))` and `1/(sqrt(x) + sqrt(y))` and their combinations, where x and y are natural numbers and a, b are integers.

6. **Laws of Exponents**: Recall of laws of exponents with integral powers. Rational exponents with positive real bases (explored through particular cases).

## Key Formulas/Theorems

**Laws of Exponents** (for positive real base a, rational exponents):
- a^m * a^n = a^(m+n)
- a^m / a^n = a^(m-n)
- (a^m)^n = a^(mn)
- a^0 = 1 (a != 0)
- a^(-m) = 1/a^m

**Rationalization Identities**:
- (a + b*sqrt(c))(a - b*sqrt(c)) = a^2 - b^2*c
- (sqrt(a) + sqrt(b))(sqrt(a) - sqrt(b)) = a - b

**Real Number Completeness**: Every point on the number line corresponds to a unique real number, and every real number corresponds to a unique point on the number line.
