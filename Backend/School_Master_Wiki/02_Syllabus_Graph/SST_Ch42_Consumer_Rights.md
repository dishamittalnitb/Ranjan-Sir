## Concept Summary / Key Points
1. **Who is a Consumer?**: A person who buys goods or services for personal use. Consumers participate in the market by purchasing products and services.
2. **Consumer Exploitation**: Consumers can be exploited through:
   - Adulterated and defective goods.
   - Overpricing (higher than MRP).
   - Misleading advertisements.
   - Underweight/under-measurement.
   - Substandard quality products.
   - Deficiency in services.
3. **Need for Consumer Protection**: To protect consumers from exploitation, ensure safety, provide redressal mechanisms, and create awareness. The consumer is often in a weak position compared to the seller.
4. **Consumer Movement in India**:
   - Consumer awareness grew in the 1960s-70s due to food shortages, hoarding, and black marketing.
   - Consumer Protection Act (COPRA) was enacted in 1986 and replaced by the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
   - 24 December is observed as National Consumer Day.
5. **Consumer Protection Act, 2019**:
   - **Three-tier quasi-judicial machinery**:
     - **District Consumer Disputes Redressal Forum (DCDRF)**: For claims up to Rs 1 crore.
     - **State Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (SCDRC)**: For claims between Rs 1 crore and Rs 10 crore.
     - **National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (NCDRC)**: For claims above Rs 10 crore.
   - **Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA)**: Established to promote, protect, and enforce consumer rights.
6. **Consumer Rights**:
   - **Right to Safety**: Protection against hazardous goods and services.
   - **Right to be Informed**: Information about quality, quantity, purity, price, and standard of goods/services (including mandatory labelling).
   - **Right to Choose**: Access to a variety of goods and services at competitive prices.
   - **Right to be Heard**: Consumer interests to be considered in appropriate forums.
   - **Right to Seek Redressal**: Fair settlement of grievances.
   - **Right to Consumer Education**: Awareness about rights and remedies.
7. **Consumer Responsibilities**: Be aware of rights, verify quality marks (ISI, Agmark, FSSAI, Hallmark), demand bills/cash memos, avoid misleading ads, and file complaints when rights are violated.

## Key Terms and Definitions
- **Consumer**: Any person who buys goods or avails services for personal use.
- **MRP (Maximum Retail Price)**: The highest price at which a product can be sold to consumers (inclusive of taxes).
- **COPRA (Consumer Protection Act)**: A law enacted to protect consumer interests and provide a redressal mechanism.
- **Adulteration**: The act of mixing inferior/substandard materials with food or other products.
- **Deficiency in Service**: Any shortcoming, imperfection, or inadequacy in the quality of service.
- **ISI Mark**: Certification mark by Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) for industrial products.
- **Agmark**: Certification for agricultural products.
- **FSSAI**: Food Safety and Standards Authority of India, responsible for food safety certification.
- **Hallmark**: Certification for gold and silver jewellery purity.
- **CCPA (Central Consumer Protection Authority)**: A regulatory body to protect and enforce consumer rights.
