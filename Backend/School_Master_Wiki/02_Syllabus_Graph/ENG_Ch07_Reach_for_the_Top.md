# Chapter 07: Reach for the Top

## Theme & Summary

**Theme:** The chapter is divided into two biographical sketches celebrating female empowerment, determination, and the will to overcome all odds. Part I chronicles Santosh Yadav, the first woman in the world to climb Mount Everest twice. Part II narrates the rise of tennis star Maria Sharapova.

**Summary (Part I - Santosh Yadav):** Born the sixth child (and only daughter to survive) in a conservative family in Rewari district, Haryana, Santosh defied all societal expectations. She fought against her parents' decision to marry her off at sixteen by threatening to fund her own education. She left home, studied in Delhi, and while at a hostel in Jaipur, watched mountaineers from her room and decided to join them. She trained at the Nehru Institute of Mountaineering, Uttarkashi, and went on to scale Everest twice — first in 1992 (at just 20) and again in 1993 as a member of an Indo-Nepalese women's expedition. She was honored with the Padma Shri.

**Summary (Part II - Maria Sharapova):** Born in Siberia, Russia, Maria Sharapova showed tennis talent at an early age. At nine, her father took her to the United States for professional training; her mother could not accompany them due to visa issues. Separated from her mother for two years, young Maria fought loneliness through sheer hard work and determination. She rose to become the world number one tennis player, winning Wimbledon in 2004 at age 17. Her mantra is that sacrifice, discipline, and relentless effort lead to success.

## Character List

- **Santosh Yadav:** Tenacious mountaineer from Haryana; defied traditions, scaled Everest twice; recipient of Padma Shri.
- **Santosh's Parents:** Initially conservative and opposed to her education; later proud of her achievements.
- **Maria Sharapova:** Siberian-born tennis champion; left home at nine for training in the USA; Wimbledon champion and world number one.
- **Yuri Sharapov (Maria's Father):** Sacrificed much to support Maria's tennis career, taking her to the USA at age nine.

## Key Quotes

1. "I used to watch the villagers from my room, going up the Aravalli hills and vanishing after a while." - Santosh
2. "I knew that if I had to achieve something, I had to fight for it." - Santosh
3. "I am very, very competitive. I work hard at what I do. It is my job." - Maria
4. "No matter what the odds are, if you have the desire and the willingness to work hard, you can achieve anything."
