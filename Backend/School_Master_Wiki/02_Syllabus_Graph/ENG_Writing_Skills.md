# English Writing Skills Reference (Classes IX & X)

## Overview

Writing Skills are assessed in **Section B (Writing Skills and Grammar, 10 marks)** for both classes. This section tests creative expression, appropriate style and tone, correct format, and fluency.

---

## CLASS IX Writing Skills

### 1. Descriptive Paragraph (5 marks)
- **Word Limit:** 100-120 words
- **Type:** Describing a person / event / situation
- **Input:** Based on visual or verbal cue(s)
- **Format:** One out of two questions to be answered
- **Assessment Criteria:**
  - Observational skills and sensory detail
  - Logical organization of ideas
  - Appropriate vocabulary and tone
  - Grammatical accuracy and spelling

**Tips:** Use vivid adjectives, appeal to the five senses (sight, sound, smell, taste, touch), maintain a consistent point of view, and structure the paragraph with a clear beginning, middle, and end.

### 2. Story Writing / Diary Entry (5 marks)
- **Word Limit:** 100-120 words
- **Type:** Writing a story (on a given cue/title) OR a diary entry
- **Format:** One out of two questions to be answered
- **Assessment Criteria:**
  - Creativity and originality
  - Coherent plot or narrative flow
  - Appropriate use of dialogue (for stories)
  - Personal and reflective tone (for diary entries)
  - Grammatical accuracy

**Story Writing Tips:** Include a clear beginning (setting and characters), middle (conflict/problem), and end (resolution). Use dialogue sparingly but effectively. Maintain a consistent point of view.

**Diary Entry Tips:** Use first-person narration, include date/day/time, express personal thoughts and feelings, be honest and reflective, maintain a conversational tone.

## CLASS X Writing Skills

### 1. Formal Letter (5 marks)
- **Word Limit:** 100-120 words
- **Type:** Formal letter based on a given situation
- **Format:** One out of two questions to be answered
- **Letter Types:**
  - Letter to the Editor (newspaper/magazine)
  - Complaint letter (to officials/authorities)
  - Enquiry letter (seeking information)
  - Order/Booking letter
  - Letter of application (job/school)

**Standard Format:**
```
Sender's Address
Date

Receiver's Designation/Address (if applicable)

Subject: (Brief, clear, and relevant)

Salutation: Sir/Madam

Body (3 paragraphs):
  Para 1: Introduction and purpose of writing
  Para 2: Details, arguments, reasons, or explanation
  Para 3: Conclusion, suggestion, or request

Complimentary Close: Yours faithfully/sincerely
Signature: (Name)
(Designation, if any)
```

**Assessment Criteria:**
- Correct format (sender's address, date, receiver's address, subject, salutation, body, closing, signature)
- Clarity of purpose and content
- Appropriate formal tone and register
- Grammatical accuracy and spelling

### 2. Analytical Paragraph (5 marks)
- **Word Limit:** 100-120 words
- **Type:** Analysis of a Map / Chart / Graph / Cue(s)
- **Format:** One out of two questions to be answered
- **Assessment Criteria:**
  - Accurate interpretation of data/visual input
  - Logical organization of information
  - Comparative analysis (trends, patterns, differences)
  - Objective and factual tone
  - Grammatical accuracy and spelling

**Structure:**
1. **Introduction:** What the chart/graph depicts (title, time period, variables)
2. **Body:** Key trends, comparisons, significant data points, patterns
3. **Conclusion:** Summary, overall trend, or inference (no personal opinion)

**Useful Language for Analytical Paragraph:**
- To introduce: "The given chart/graph illustrates..." / "The data provides information about..."
- To compare: "In comparison to..." / "On the other hand..." / "While... whereas..."
- To describe trends: "increased dramatically," "declined steadily," "remained constant," "peaked at," "fluctuated," "plateaued"
- To conclude: "Overall..." / "The data clearly indicates..." / "It is evident from the chart that..."

## Common Assessment Rubric for Writing Skills

Both Class IX and X writing tasks are evaluated on:
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Content (relevance, completeness) | 2 marks |
| Organization (structure, coherence) | 1 mark |
| Expression (vocabulary, style) | 1 mark |
| Accuracy (grammar, spelling, punctuation) | 1 mark |
| **Total** | **5 marks** |

---

## Additional Syllabus Notes

- The syllabus for secondary level also expects students to develop longer writing skills including articles, speeches, debates, reports, and summary writing (as per background objectives). However, the board examination focuses on the specific formats listed above.
- Teachers are encouraged to practice all formats through classroom activities, even if only specified formats appear in the final exam.

## Internal Assessment

Writing skills indirectly contribute to Internal Assessment through:
- **Art-integrated projects** (scripts for skits, role-plays, dramatizations)
- **Listening and Speaking Skills (ALS, 5 marks)** — where grammatical accuracy and fluency in oral expression are assessed
