# Chapter 10: Heron's Formula (Class IX)

## Concept Summary

This chapter presents Heron's formula for finding the area of a triangle without using height:

1. **Heron's Formula** (without proof): Area of a triangle in terms of its three sides. If a, b, c are the sides of a triangle, then:
   - Semi-perimeter: s = (a + b + c) / 2
   - Area = sqrt(s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c))

2. **Applications**: Finding area of triangles when altitude is not conveniently available. Useful for scalene triangles and problems involving triangular plots/fields.

3. **Verification**: The formula can be verified for special triangles (equilateral, right-angled) using standard area formulas.

## Key Formulas/Theorems

**Heron's Formula**:
- s = (a + b + c) / 2
- Area = sqrt(s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c))

**For Equilateral Triangle** (side a):
- s = 3a/2
- Area = (sqrt(3)/4) * a^2

**Relation to Standard Formula**: Area = (1/2) * base * height (for verification purposes)
