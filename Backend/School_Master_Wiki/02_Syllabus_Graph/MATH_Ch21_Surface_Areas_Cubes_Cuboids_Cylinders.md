# Chapter 21: Surface Areas - Cubes, Cuboids, Cylinders [FORMATIVE ONLY]

## Concept Summary

This formative-only topic covers surface areas and volumes of basic 3D solids:

1. **Cube**: A solid with six equal square faces. All edges equal (side = a).

2. **Cuboid**: A solid with six rectangular faces. Three dimensions: length (l), breadth (b), height (h).

3. **Right Circular Cylinder**: A solid generated by rotating a rectangle about one of its sides. Base is a circle of radius r; height is h.

4. **Surface Areas**: Curved Surface Area (CSA) and Total Surface Area (TSA).

5. **Volumes**: Space occupied by the solid.

## Key Formulas/Theorems

**Cube** (edge = a):
- Lateral Surface Area (LSA) = 4a^2
- Total Surface Area (TSA) = 6a^2
- Volume = a^3
- Diagonal = a * sqrt(3)

**Cuboid** (length = l, breadth = b, height = h):
- Lateral Surface Area (LSA) = 2h(l + b)
- Total Surface Area (TSA) = 2(lb + bh + hl)
- Volume = l * b * h
- Diagonal = sqrt(l^2 + b^2 + h^2)

**Right Circular Cylinder** (radius = r, height = h):
- Curved Surface Area (CSA) = 2 * pi * r * h
- Total Surface Area (TSA) = 2 * pi * r * (h + r)
- Volume = pi * r^2 * h
