# Chapter 09: Circles (Class IX)

## Concept Summary

This chapter explores the geometry of circles:

1. **Basic Definitions**: Circle, center, radius, diameter, chord, arc, major/minor arc, sector, segment, circumference, secant, tangent, subtended angle.

2. **Equal Chords and Angles** (provable): Equal chords of a circle subtend equal angles at the center. (Converse also true, without proof.)

3. **Perpendicular from Center to Chord** (stated without proof): The perpendicular from the center of a circle to a chord bisects the chord. Conversely, the line drawn through the center to bisect a chord is perpendicular to the chord.

4. **Equal Chords and Distance** (stated without proof): Equal chords of a circle (or of congruent circles) are equidistant from the center (or their respective centers). (Converse also true.)

5. **Angle at Center Theorem** (provable): The angle subtended by an arc at the center is double the angle subtended by it at any point on the remaining part of the circle.

6. **Angles in Same Segment** (stated without proof): Angles in the same segment of a circle are equal.

7. **Concyclic Points** (stated without proof): If a line segment joining two points subtends equal angles at two other points lying on the same side of the line containing the segment, the four points lie on a circle.

8. **Cyclic Quadrilaterals** (stated without proof): The sum of either pair of opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral is 180 degrees. (Converse also true.)

## Key Formulas/Theorems

**Angle at Center**: Angle AOB = 2 * Angle APB (where P is any point on the remaining part)

**Angles in Same Segment**: Angle APB = Angle AQB (P, Q in the same segment)

**Cyclic Quadrilateral**: Angle A + Angle C = 180 degrees, Angle B + Angle D = 180 degrees.

**Chord Properties**:
- Equal chords -> equal distances from center
- Equal chords -> equal angles at center
- Perpendicular from center to chord bisects it
