# Chapter 31: Circles (Class X)

## Concept Summary

This chapter studies tangents to circles:

1. **Tangent to a Circle**: A line that touches the circle at exactly one point, called the point of contact. A secant is a line that intersects the circle at two points.

2. **Tangent-Perpendicular Theorem** (provable): The tangent at any point of a circle is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact.

3. **Tangents from External Point** (provable): The lengths of tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal.

4. **Number of Tangents**: 
   - From a point on the circle: exactly one tangent
   - From a point inside the circle: zero tangents
   - From a point outside the circle: exactly two tangents

5. **Applications**: Proving geometric relationships involving circles and tangents, construction of tangents to a circle from an external point.

## Key Formulas/Theorems

**Theorem 1**: The tangent at any point of a circle is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact.
- If PT is tangent at point T, then OT is perpendicular to PT (O is center).

**Theorem 2**: The lengths of tangents drawn from an external point P to a circle are equal.
- PA = PB (where A and B are points of contact of tangents from P)

**Number of Tangents from a Point**:
- Point inside circle: 0 tangents
- Point on circle: 1 tangent
- Point outside circle: 2 tangents
