# Chapter 08: Motion

## 1. Concept Summary

### 1.1 Rest and Motion
- An object is said to be at rest if its position does not change with time with respect to a reference point.
- An object is said to be in motion if its position changes with time with respect to a reference point.
- Motion is relative; an object may appear at rest to one observer and in motion to another.

### 1.2 Distance and Displacement
| Quantity | Distance | Displacement |
|----------|----------|--------------|
| **Definition** | Total path length covered by an object | Shortest straight-line distance between initial and final position |
| **Type** | Scalar (magnitude only) | Vector (magnitude + direction) |
| **Value** | Always positive | Can be positive, negative, or zero |
| **Relation** | Distance >= Displacement | Displacement <= Distance |

### 1.3 Speed and Velocity
| Quantity | Speed | Velocity |
|----------|-------|----------|
| **Definition** | Distance covered per unit time | Displacement per unit time |
| **Formula** | Speed = Distance / Time | Velocity = Displacement / Time |
| **Type** | Scalar | Vector |
| **SI Unit** | m/s (metre per second) | m/s (metre per second) |

- **Average Speed:** Total distance travelled / Total time taken.
- **Average Velocity:** Total displacement / Total time taken.
- **Instantaneous Speed/ Velocity:** Speed/velocity at a particular instant of time.

### 1.4 Uniform and Non-Uniform Motion
- **Uniform motion:** Equal distances covered in equal intervals of time (constant speed).
- **Non-uniform motion:** Unequal distances covered in equal intervals of time (variable speed).

### 1.5 Acceleration
- Rate of change of velocity with time.
  - Acceleration (a) = (Change in velocity) / Time = (v - u) / t
  - SI unit: m/s^2
- **Positive acceleration:** Velocity increases (v > u).
- **Negative acceleration (Deceleration/Retardation):** Velocity decreases (v < u).
- **Uniform acceleration:** Equal changes in velocity in equal intervals of time (e.g., acceleration due to gravity near earth's surface).
- **Non-uniform acceleration:** Unequal changes in velocity in equal intervals of time.

### 1.6 Equations of Motion (Uniformly Accelerated Motion)
For uniform acceleration 'a' along a straight line:
| Eq. | Formula | Variables |
|-----|---------|-----------|
| **1st Equation** | v = u + at | Final velocity (v), Initial velocity (u), acceleration (a), time (t) |
| **2nd Equation** | s = ut + (1/2)at^2 | Displacement (s) |
| **3rd Equation** | 2as = v^2 - u^2 | Relating v, u, a, s (without t) |

### 1.7 Graphical Representation of Motion
#### Distance-Time Graphs
- **Uniform motion:** Straight line with constant slope. Slope = speed.
- **Non-uniform motion:** Curved line; slope changes with time.
- **Object at rest:** Horizontal straight line (parallel to time axis).

#### Velocity-Time Graphs
- **Uniform velocity:** Horizontal straight line parallel to time axis.
- **Uniform acceleration:** Straight line with constant slope. Slope = acceleration. Area under graph = displacement.
- **Non-uniform acceleration:** Curved line.

### 1.8 Uniform Circular Motion
- Motion of an object along a circular path with constant speed.
- Velocity changes at every point due to change in direction (though magnitude of speed is constant).
- Acceleration is directed toward the centre (centripetal acceleration).
- Examples: Earth revolving around the Sun, a stone tied to a thread and whirled, a cyclist on a circular track.

## 2. Key Formulas/Definitions

| Term | Formula |
|------|---------|
| **Speed** | v = s/t (distance/time) |
| **Velocity** | v = s/t (displacement/time) |
| **Average Speed** | Total distance / Total time |
| **Acceleration** | a = (v - u)/t |
| **1st Equation of Motion** | v = u + at |
| **2nd Equation of Motion** | s = ut + (1/2)at^2 |
| **3rd Equation of Motion** | v^2 - u^2 = 2as |
| **Distance (v-t graph)** | Area under velocity-time graph |
| **Centripetal Acceleration** | a_c = v^2 / r (r = radius of circular path) |

## 3. Board Exam Weightage & Question Pattern

Falls under **Unit III: Motion, Force and Work (27 marks)** - the highest-weightage unit in Class IX.

- **VSA (1 mark):** Define displacement, uniform motion; give example of uniform circular motion; identify scalar/vector.
- **SA (2-3 marks):** Differentiate distance and displacement; differentiate speed and velocity; interpret s-t and v-t graphs; derive equation of motion.
- **LA (5 marks):** Solve numerical using equations of motion; plot and interpret graphs; derive v-t relations.
- **Numerical Problems:** Very important - based on all three equations of motion, average speed calculations, graph interpretation.
- **Assertion-Reasoning:** Based on distance vs displacement, uniform vs non-uniform motion.
