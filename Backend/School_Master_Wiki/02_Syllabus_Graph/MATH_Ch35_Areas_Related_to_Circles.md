# Chapter 35: Areas Related to Circles (Class X)

## Concept Summary

This chapter studies areas of sectors and segments of a circle:

1. **Review**: Area of circle = pi * r^2, Circumference = 2 * pi * r

2. **Sector**: The region enclosed by two radii and the corresponding arc.
   - Area of sector with central angle theta degrees = (theta/360) * pi * r^2
   - Length of arc with central angle theta degrees = (theta/360) * 2 * pi * r

3. **Segment**: The region enclosed by a chord and the corresponding arc.
   - Minor segment: smaller region
   - Major segment: larger region
   - Area of minor segment = Area of sector - Area of corresponding triangle
   - Area of major segment = Area of circle - Area of minor segment

4. **Restrictions**: Problems on segment area should be restricted to central angles of 60 degrees, 90 degrees, and 120 degrees only.

5. **Combined Figures**: Problems involving combinations of plane figures (semicircle, quadrant, annulus, etc.).

## Key Formulas/Theorems

**Circle**:
- Circumference = 2 * pi * r
- Area = pi * r^2

**Sector** (angle theta degrees):
- Arc length = (theta/360) * 2 * pi * r
- Area = (theta/360) * pi * r^2

**Segment** (angle 60 degrees):
- Area = (pi * r^2 / 6) - (sqrt(3)/4) * r^2

**Segment** (angle 90 degrees):
- Area = (pi * r^2 / 4) - (r^2 / 2)

**Segment** (angle 120 degrees):
- Area = (pi * r^2 / 3) - (sqrt(3)/4) * r^2
