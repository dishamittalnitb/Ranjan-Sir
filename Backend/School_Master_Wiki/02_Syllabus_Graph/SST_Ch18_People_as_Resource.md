# Chapter 18: People as Resource

## Learning Outcomes
The students will be able to:
- Evaluate the reasons that contribute to the quality of population.
- Observe different government schemes and see their effect on the people.

## Concept Summary / Key Points
1. **People as Resource**: The concept that people are an asset (human capital) rather than a liability. When investment is made in education, health, and training, the population becomes productive "human resource."
2. **Human Capital Formation**: The process of adding to the stock of human capital. Investments include:
   - **Education**: Builds skills, knowledge, and productivity.
   - **Health**: Improves physical capacity, reduces absenteeism.
   - **Training**: On-the-job and vocational skills.
   - **Migration**: Exposure to new skills and opportunities.
3. **Economic Activities**: Divided into three sectors:
   - **Primary**: Agriculture, fishing, forestry, mining.
   - **Secondary**: Manufacturing, construction.
   - **Tertiary**: Services like banking, education, transport.
4. **Quality of Population**: Determined by:
   - **Literacy Rate**: 74.04% overall (2011); significant gap between male (82.14%) and female (65.46%).
   - **Health Status**: Measured by life expectancy, infant mortality rate, availability of hospitals.
   - **Skill Formation**: Technical and vocational training.
5. **Unemployment**: A situation where people willing and able to work do not find jobs. Types:
   - **Seasonal Unemployment**: Agricultural labourers without work during off-season.
   - **Disguised Unemployment**: More people engaged than needed; removing some would not reduce output (common in agriculture).
   - **Educated Unemployment**: Graduates unable to find suitable jobs, common in urban areas.
6. **Government Schemes**: Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan (universal elementary education), Mid-Day Meal Scheme, National Health Mission, Skill India Mission, MNREGA (rural employment guarantee).

## Key Terms and Definitions
- **Human Capital**: The stock of knowledge, skills, and health embodied in people that enables productivity.
- **Gross National Product (GNP)**: Total value of goods and services produced by a country's residents.
- **Labour Force**: All persons who are working or seeking work.
- **Disguised Unemployment**: A situation where more people are working than actually required; marginal productivity is near zero.
- **Seasonal Unemployment**: Unemployment during particular seasons, common in agriculture.
- **Life Expectancy**: The average number of years a person is expected to live.
- **Infant Mortality Rate (IMR)**: Number of infant deaths per 1000 live births.
- **Literacy Rate**: Percentage of people aged 7+ who can read and write.
- **Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan (SSA)**: A government programme for universal elementary education.
