# Chapter 02: The Sound of Music

## Theme & Summary

**Theme:** The chapter (divided into two parts) celebrates the triumph of human spirit over physical limitations and the universality of music. Part I focuses on Evelyn Glennie, a profoundly deaf percussionist who feels music through vibrations. Part II celebrates Bismillah Khan, the shehnai maestro who elevated a folk instrument to classical status and embodied India's composite culture.

**Summary (Part I - Evelyn Glennie):** Evelyn Glennie lost her hearing at age eleven but was determined to pursue music. With guidance from percussionist Ron Forbes, she learned to sense musical notes through vibrations in different parts of her body. She mastered over 1000 percussion instruments, became a world-renowned solo percussionist, and earned countless awards, proving that disability is no barrier to achievement.

**Summary (Part II - Bismillah Khan):** Born in a family of musicians in Bihar, Bismillah Khan took the shehnai from temples and wedding processions to the classical concert stage. His performance at the Red Fort on India's Independence Day (15 August 1947) became historic. Despite global fame and an invitation to set up a music school in the USA, he remained deeply attached to Benaras and the Ganga.

## Character List

- **Evelyn Glennie:** A profoundly deaf percussionist from Scotland; determined, hardworking, and an inspiration to millions.
- **Ron Forbes:** Evelyn's first percussion teacher who taught her to feel music through vibrations.
- **Bismillah Khan:** The legendary shehnai player; Padma Vibhushan and Bharat Ratna awardee; deeply patriotic and rooted in Indian tradition.
- **Ali Bux:** Bismillah Khan's maternal uncle who mentored him in shehnai playing at the Vishnu temple in Benaras.

## Key Quotes

1. "God may have taken her hearing but He has given her back something extraordinary."
2. "If you work hard and know where you are going, you'll get there."
3. "Music has no caste, no religion."
4. "Teach your children music; this is the best education."
