# Chapter 15: Lines and Angles Extras [FORMATIVE ONLY]

## Concept Summary

This formative-only topic extends Lines and Angles with additional results:

1. **Results on Corresponding Angles** (stated without proof): When a transversal intersects two parallel lines, corresponding angles are equal. Conversely, if corresponding angles are equal, the lines are parallel.

2. **Results on Alternate Angles** (stated without proof): When a transversal intersects two parallel lines, alternate interior angles are equal. Conversely, if alternate interior angles are equal, the lines are parallel.

3. **Results on Interior Angles** (stated without proof): When a transversal intersects two parallel lines, interior angles on the same side (co-interior/consecutive interior angles) are supplementary (sum = 180 degrees). Conversely applies.

4. **Angle Sum of a Triangle** (provable): The sum of the three angles of a triangle is 180 degrees.

5. **Exterior Angle Property** (stated without proof): If a side of a triangle is produced, the exterior angle so formed equals the sum of the two interior opposite angles.

## Key Formulas/Theorems

**Transversal-Parallel Lines Angle Relations**:
- Corresponding angles: Angle 1 = Angle 5, etc.
- Alternate interior: Angle 3 = Angle 5, Angle 4 = Angle 6
- Co-interior: Angle 3 + Angle 6 = 180 degrees, Angle 4 + Angle 5 = 180 degrees

**Angle Sum of Triangle**: In triangle ABC, Angle A + Angle B + Angle C = 180 degrees

**Exterior Angle**: Exterior Angle ACD = Angle A + Angle B (for triangle ABC with BC extended to D)
