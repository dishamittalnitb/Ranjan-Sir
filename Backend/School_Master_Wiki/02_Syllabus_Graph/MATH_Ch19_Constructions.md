# Chapter 19: Constructions [FORMATIVE ONLY]

## Concept Summary

This formative-only topic covers geometric constructions using ruler and compass:

1. **Basic Constructions**: 
   - Bisector of a given line segment (perpendicular bisector)
   - Bisector of a given angle
   - Construction of angles: 60 degrees, 90 degrees, 45 degrees, etc.
   - Construction of an equilateral triangle

2. **Triangle Construction**: Construction of a triangle given its base, sum/difference of the other two sides, and one base angle.

3. **Method and Justification**: Each construction must be accompanied by geometric reasoning (why the construction yields the correct result).

## Key Formulas/Theorems

**Perpendicular Bisector Construction**:
1. With A and B as centers, draw arcs of equal radius > AB/2 on both sides of AB
2. The line joining the intersection points of these arcs is the perpendicular bisector

**Angle Bisector Construction**:
1. With vertex as center, draw an arc intersecting both arms
2. From these intersection points, draw arcs of equal radius intersecting within the angle
3. Join vertex to this intersection point

**Angle Constructions**: 
- 60 degrees: Construct an equilateral triangle
- 90 degrees: Construct perpendicular to a line through a point
- 45 degrees: Bisect 90 degrees
- 30 degrees: Bisect 60 degrees

**Triangle Construction** (given base, sum/difference, base angle): Procedure varies by case; involves constructing an auxiliary isosceles triangle.
