# Chapter 06: My Childhood

## Theme & Summary

**Theme:** This autobiographical extract from Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam's "Wings of Fire" celebrates the values of communal harmony, religious tolerance, and the role of nurturing family and teachers in shaping a child's future. It underlines that a supportive environment can transcend social barriers.

**Summary:** Dr. Kalam recounts his childhood in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. Born into a middle-class Tamil Muslim family, his father Jainulabdeen was a boat owner and imam at the local mosque, while his mother Ashiamma was a generous homemaker. Kalam's early life was marked by simplicity, discipline, and religious harmony — his three closest friends were from Hindu Brahmin families. His science teacher, Sivasubramania Iyer, invited Kalam for a meal at his home, breaking orthodox caste barriers; Iyer's wife was initially hesitant but later served Kalam herself, showing that change is possible. Kalam also earned his first wages distributing newspapers while studying. The chapter shows how diverse influences — his father's wisdom, his mother's kindness, and his teachers' encouragement — shaped the "Missile Man of India."

## Character List

- **APJ Abdul Kalam (narrator):** The future President of India as a young boy; curious, hardworking, and shaped by multiple religious traditions.
- **Jainulabdeen:** Kalam's father; a wise, self-disciplined imam and boat owner who taught Kalam the power of simplicity and prayer.
- **Ashiamma:** Kalam's mother; kind-hearted and generous, feeding many people daily.
- **Sivasubramania Iyer:** Kalam's science teacher; progressive and bold, defied caste norms by inviting Kalam home for a meal.
- **Iyer's Wife:** Initially hesitant to serve a Muslim boy, but eventually feeds him herself, symbolizing the triumph of humanity over prejudice.
- **Ramanadha Sastry, Aravindan, Sivaprakasan:** Kalam's three close Hindu friends during childhood in Rameswaram.

## Key Quotes

1. "Every child is born, in some way, a genius."
2. "I inherited honesty and self-discipline from my father; from my mother, I inherited faith in goodness and deep kindness."
3. "When you pray, you transcend your body and become a part of the cosmos."
4. "Once you decide to change the system, such problems have to be confronted."
