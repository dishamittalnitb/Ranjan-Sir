# Chapter 16: Triangle Inequalities [FORMATIVE ONLY]

## Concept Summary

This formative-only topic explores inequalities within triangles:

1. **Triangle Inequalities** (stated without proof): The sum of any two sides of a triangle is greater than the third side.
   - AB + BC > AC
   - BC + AC > AB
   - AC + AB > BC

2. **Angle-Side Relationship** (stated without proof): In a triangle, the angle opposite the longer side is larger (and the side opposite the larger angle is longer).
   - If AB > AC, then Angle C > Angle B
   - If Angle C > Angle B, then AB > AC

3. **Applications**: 
   - Determining if three given lengths can form a triangle
   - Comparing angles when side lengths are known
   - Ordering sides based on given angles

## Key Formulas/Theorems

**Triangle Inequality**: For any triangle with sides a, b, c:
- a + b > c
- b + c > a
- c + a > b

**Angle-Side Relationship**: In triangle ABC:
- Side opposite to larger angle is longer
- Angle opposite to longer side is larger

**Consequences**:
- The hypotenuse is the longest side in a right triangle
- In an obtuse triangle, the side opposite the obtuse angle is the longest
