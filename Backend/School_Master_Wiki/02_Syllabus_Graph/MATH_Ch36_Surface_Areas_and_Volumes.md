# Chapter 36: Surface Areas and Volumes (Class X)

## Concept Summary

This chapter extends mensuration to combinations of solids:

1. **Combinations of Solids**: Problems involving surface areas and volumes of combinations of any two of the following solids:
   - Cubes
   - Cuboids
   - Spheres
   - Hemispheres
   - Right circular cylinders
   - Right circular cones

2. **Typical Combinations**:
   - A cone on top of a cylinder (e.g., a tent)
   - A hemisphere on top of a cylinder (e.g., a capsule-shaped container)
   - A cone on top of a hemisphere (e.g., an ice-cream shape)
   - A cylinder drilled out of a sphere
   - A cone mounted on a cube
   - A frustum-like shape from a cone cut by a plane (introduction, but frustum is not in syllabus)

3. **Conversion of Solids**: When one solid is melted and recast into another, the volume remains constant.

4. **Practical Applications**: Capacity of containers, cost of materials, painting, manufacturing.

## Key Formulas/Theorems

**Individual Solid Formulas**:

| Solid | CSA/LSA | TSA | Volume |
|-------|---------|-----|--------|
| Cube (edge a) | 4a^2 | 6a^2 | a^3 |
| Cuboid (l, b, h) | 2h(l+b) | 2(lb+bh+hl) | lbh |
| Cylinder (r, h) | 2*pi*r*h | 2*pi*r(r+h) | pi*r^2*h |
| Cone (r, h, l) | pi*r*l | pi*r(l+r) | (1/3)*pi*r^2*h |
| Sphere (r) | 4*pi*r^2 | 4*pi*r^2 | (4/3)*pi*r^3 |
| Hemisphere (r) | 2*pi*r^2 | 3*pi*r^2 | (2/3)*pi*r^3 |

**For Combinations**: 
- TSA = Sum of exposed surface areas
- Volume = Sum of individual volumes
- When melting/reshaping: Volume before = Volume after
