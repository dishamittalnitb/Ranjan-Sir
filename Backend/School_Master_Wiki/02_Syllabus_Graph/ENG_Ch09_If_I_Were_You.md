# Chapter 09: If I Were You

## Theme & Summary

**Theme:** This one-act play explores themes of wit, presence of mind, and how intelligence can triumph over brute force. It demonstrates that a calm, clever mind can outwit a physically superior opponent in a life-threatening situation.

**Summary:** The play opens with Gerrard, a playwright, packing a bag to leave his cottage. A stranger, an escaped criminal named Intruder, breaks in with a revolver. The Intruder, who resembles Gerrard, plans to kill him and assume his identity to escape the police. Gerrard, instead of panicking, remains calm and uses his wit. He casually reveals that he himself is a criminal on the run who accidentally killed a cop — and the police are about to raid the cottage. He then convinces the Intruder that the only escape route is through a cupboard door that leads to a garage. When the Intruder opens the door, Gerrard pushes him in and slams the door, locking him inside. He then calls the police. The story ends with Gerrard's final words to the trapped intruder.

## Character List

- **Gerrard:** A witty, calm, and resourceful playwright; uses intelligence to outwit a dangerous intruder. Lives alone in a secluded cottage.
- **The Intruder:** An escaped criminal; a burglar wanted for jewel robbery; intends to kill Gerrard and steal his identity. Physically strong but intellectually outmatched.

## Key Quotes

1. "At last a sympathetic audience!" - Gerrard to Intruder
2. "I'm sorry to sound stern but it's all for your good."
3. "You won't kill me for a very good reason. You are not a murderer."
4. "I said, you're not going to kill me. Not just yet."
5. "In most melodramas the villain is foolish enough to delay his killing long enough to be frustrated."
