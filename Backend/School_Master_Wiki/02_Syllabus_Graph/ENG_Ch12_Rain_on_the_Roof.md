# Chapter 12: Rain on the Roof

## Theme & Summary

**Theme:** The poem explores the soothing, nostalgic effect of rain on the human soul. It celebrates the sensory experience of rain — its sound, feel, and the memories it evokes. The central themes are nostalgia, the restorative power of nature, and the deep emotional connection between weather and human sentiment.

**Summary:** The speaker lies in his cozy bed on a rainy night, listening to the soft patter of raindrops on the shingles (roof tiles). The sound triggers a chain of memories and dreams. He recalls his childhood, especially his mother who would lovingly watch over him and his siblings as they slept. The rain, which initially brings general fancies and dreamy thoughts, ultimately converges into the most specific and tender memory — that of his mother's loving gaze. The speaker ends by reaffirming the bliss of listening to the rain.

## Literary Devices

- **Rhyme Scheme:** ABCBDEFE (alternating rhyme within each stanza)
- **Personification:** Raindrops are described as "the melancholy darkness gently weeps in rainy tears."
- **Alliteration:** "starry spheres," "dreamy fancies," "busy being," "rain upon the roof"
- **Onomatopoeia:** "patter," "tinkle" — mimicking the sound of rain.
- **Imagery:** Rich visual and auditory imagery — "starry skies," "darkness," "rain on the shingles."
- **Refrain:** "As I listen to the patter of the rain upon the roof" — repeated across stanzas.
- **Tone:** Melancholic, reflective, nostalgic.

## Key Quotes

1. "When the humid shadows hover / Over all the starry spheres"
2. "Every tinkle on the shingles / Has an echo in the heart"
3. "Now in memory comes my mother, / As she used in years agone"
4. "And a thousand dreamy fancies into busy being start"
