# Chapter 32: Introduction to Trigonometry (Class X)

## Concept Summary

This chapter introduces trigonometric ratios for right-angled triangles:

1. **Trigonometric Ratios**: For an acute angle A in a right-angled triangle:
   - sin A = Opposite/Hypotenuse
   - cos A = Adjacent/Hypotenuse
   - tan A = Opposite/Adjacent = sin A/cos A
   - cosec A = 1/sin A = Hypotenuse/Opposite
   - sec A = 1/cos A = Hypotenuse/Adjacent
   - cot A = 1/tan A = Adjacent/Opposite = cos A/sin A

2. **Proof of Existence**: The ratios are well-defined (independent of the size of the right triangle, depending only on the angle). This is proved using similarity of triangles.

3. **Ratios at 0 degrees and 90 degrees**: Motivate the values of trigonometric ratios at these boundary angles.

4. **Values for Standard Angles**:
   - 30 degrees, 45 degrees, 60 degrees
   - Derived from right isosceles triangle (45-45-90) and equilateral triangle (30-60-90)

5. **Relationships Between Ratios**:
   - tan A = sin A / cos A
   - cot A = cos A / sin A
   - sin A = 1/cosec A
   - cos A = 1/sec A
   - tan A = 1/cot A

## Key Formulas/Theorems

**Table of Standard Values**:

| Angle | sin | cos | tan | cosec | sec | cot |
|-------|-----|-----|-----|-------|-----|-----|
| 0 deg | 0 | 1 | 0 | Undefined | 1 | Undefined |
| 30 deg | 1/2 | sqrt(3)/2 | 1/sqrt(3) | 2 | 2/sqrt(3) | sqrt(3) |
| 45 deg | 1/sqrt(2) | 1/sqrt(2) | 1 | sqrt(2) | sqrt(2) | 1 |
| 60 deg | sqrt(3)/2 | 1/2 | sqrt(3) | 2/sqrt(3) | 2 | 1/sqrt(3) |
| 90 deg | 1 | 0 | Undefined | 1 | Undefined | 0 |

**Relations**:
- sin(90 - A) = cos A
- cos(90 - A) = sin A
- tan(90 - A) = cot A
