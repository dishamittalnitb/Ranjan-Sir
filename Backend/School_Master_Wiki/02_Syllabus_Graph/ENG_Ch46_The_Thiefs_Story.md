# Chapter 46: The Thief's Story

## Theme & Summary

**Theme:** The story explores themes of trust, redemption, and the transformative power of kindness and education. It shows how one person's faith and compassion can inspire a person to abandon a life of crime and choose the path of honesty and hard work.

**Summary:** Hari Singh is a 15-year-old thief who approaches a young man named Anil with the intention of robbing him. Anil, a struggling writer, takes him in, feeds him, and even teaches him to read and write. Hari works for Anil — cooking and shopping — but also secretly skims a rupee or two from the daily supplies, which Anil tolerates. One night, Hari steals Anil's money (600 rupees) and flees. But on the train platform, he finds he cannot leave — Anil's trust and kindness have changed him. He returns, puts the wet money back under the mattress, and goes to sleep. In the morning, Anil gives him a fifty-rupee note, saying he will be paid regularly now. Nothing more is said — but Hari knows Anil is aware of what happened, and has forgiven him.

## Character List

- **Hari Singh (Narrator):** A 15-year-old thief who reforms through Anil's kindness and trust.
- **Anil:** A 25-year-old struggling writer; kind, trusting, and patient; values education and gives Hari a second chance.

## Key Quotes

1. "Anil was watching a wrestling match when I approached him."
2. "He knew how much I loved the name Hari Singh."
3. "I had forgotten my education in my excitement."
4. "Anil gave me a fifty-rupee note — he knew I needed the money."
