# Chapter 01: The Fun They Had

## Theme & Summary

**Theme:** The story explores the contrast between mechanized, isolated future education and the human-centric, social schooling of the past. It raises questions about the role of technology in education and the irreplaceable value of human interaction in learning.

**Summary:** Set in the year 2157, the story follows two children, Margie and Tommy, who discover an old printed book about schools from centuries ago. Margie, who learns at home through a mechanical teacher (a computer-like device), is fascinated to learn that children once went to a common building to be taught by human teachers, studied together, and helped each other with homework. The story ends with Margie daydreaming about the "fun they had" in those old-fashioned schools.

## Character List

- **Margie Jones (Margie):** An 11-year-old girl; the protagonist. She dislikes her mechanical teacher and is curious about the old school system.
- **Tommy:** A 13-year-old boy, Margie's neighbor and friend. He finds the old book and explains to Margie how schools used to function.
- **Margie's Mother / Mrs. Jones:** Calls for the County Inspector when Margie's mechanical teacher malfunctions; believes in regular learning hours.
- **The County Inspector:** A repairman who fixes Margie's mechanical teacher; a kind, understanding man.

## Key Quotes

1. "Margie was thinking about how the kids must have loved it in the old days. She was thinking about the fun they had."
2. "They had a teacher... but it wasn't a regular teacher. It was a man."
3. "A man isn't smart enough." / "Sure he is. My father knows as much as my teacher."
4. "The part Margie hated most was the slot where she had to put homework and test papers."
