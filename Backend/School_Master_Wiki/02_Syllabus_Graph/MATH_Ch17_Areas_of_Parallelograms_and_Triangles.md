# Chapter 17: Areas of Parallelograms and Triangles [FORMATIVE ONLY]

## Concept Summary

This formative-only topic studies area relationships between parallelograms and triangles:

1. **Review**: Recall area of a rectangle (length * breadth) and its relationship to area of a parallelogram (base * height).

2. **Parallelograms on Same Base** (provable): Parallelograms on the same base and between the same parallels are equal in area.

3. **Triangles on Same Base** (stated without proof): Triangles on the same base (or equal bases) and between the same parallels are equal in area.

4. **Relationship**: A triangle and a parallelogram on the same base and between the same parallels -- the area of the triangle is half the area of the parallelogram.

5. **Applications**: 
   - Finding areas of complex figures by decomposing into triangles and parallelograms
   - Proving area equalities

## Key Formulas/Theorems

**Area Formulas**:
- Rectangle: Area = length * breadth
- Parallelogram: Area = base * height
- Triangle: Area = (1/2) * base * height

**Equal Area Theorems**:
- Parallelograms on same base and between same parallels are equal in area.
- Triangles on same base and between same parallels are equal in area.

**Parallelogram-Triangle Relation**: If a triangle and a parallelogram are on the same base and between the same parallels, Area of triangle = (1/2) * Area of parallelogram.
