# Chapter 08: Drainage

## Learning Outcomes
The students will be able to:
- Examine the information about different lakes and infer their contribution to Indian ecology.
- Present creative solutions to overcome water pollution and also to increase the contribution of water bodies to the Indian economy.
- Identify the river systems of the country and explain the role of rivers in human society.

## Concept Summary / Key Points
1. **Drainage System**: The network of rivers and streams that drain water from a geographical area. India's drainage system is broadly divided into the Himalayan Rivers and the Peninsular Rivers.
2. **Himalayan Rivers**: Perennial rivers fed by rain and snowmelt. Major systems:
   - **Indus System**: Originates near Lake Mansarovar (Tibet). Tributaries include Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas, and Sutlej. Flows through Pakistan. Major dams: Bhakra Nangal (Sutlej).
   - **Ganga System**: Originates as Bhagirathi from Gangotri glacier, joined by Alaknanda at Devaprayag. Major tributaries: Yamuna, Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi, Son. Forms the world's largest delta (Sundarbans) with Brahmaputra.
   - **Brahmaputra System**: Originates in Tibet (as Tsangpo). Enters India in Arunachal Pradesh. Joins Ganga in Bangladesh forming the Sundarbans delta.
3. **Peninsular Rivers**: Mostly seasonal, rain-fed rivers. Two categories:
   - **West-flowing rivers (into Arabian Sea)**: Narmada (flows through rift valley), Tapti. Form estuaries.
   - **East-flowing rivers (into Bay of Bengal)**: Mahanadi, Godavari (largest peninsular river, also called Dakshin Ganga), Krishna, Kaveri. Form deltas.
4. **Lakes of India**: Major lakes include:
   - Wular Lake (J&K) -- largest freshwater lake in India
   - Sambhar Lake (Rajasthan) -- largest inland salt lake
   - Chilika Lake (Odisha) -- largest brackish water lagoon
   - Pulicat Lake (Tamil Nadu/Andhra Pradesh) -- second largest brackish water lagoon
5. **Role of Rivers**: Irrigation, hydroelectric power generation, navigation, water supply, fisheries, environmental balance.
6. **River Pollution**: Caused by industrial effluents, domestic sewage, agricultural run-off, and religious practices. Measures needed: sewage treatment, industrial regulation, afforestation, public awareness.

## Key Terms and Definitions
- **Drainage Basin**: The area drained by a river and its tributaries.
- **Perennial River**: A river that flows throughout the year (e.g., Himalayan rivers).
- **Tributary**: A stream or river that flows into a larger river.
- **Distributary**: A branch of a river that flows away from the main channel (e.g., in deltas).
- **Delta**: A triangular landform at a river's mouth, formed by sediment deposition.
- **Estuary**: A partially enclosed coastal body of water where river meets sea (not forming a delta).
- **Rift Valley**: A valley formed by the sinking of land between parallel faults (e.g., Narmada and Tapti valleys).
- **Water Divide**: An elevated area (mountain/hill) that separates two drainage basins.
