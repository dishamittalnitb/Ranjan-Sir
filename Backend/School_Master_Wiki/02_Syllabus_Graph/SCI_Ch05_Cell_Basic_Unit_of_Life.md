# Chapter 05: Cell - Basic Unit of Life

## 1. Concept Summary

### 1.1 Cell as the Basic Unit of Life
- The cell is the structural and functional unit of all living organisms.
- Discovered by Robert Hooke (1665) in cork slices.
- All living organisms are composed of cells (unicellular or multicellular).

### 1.2 Types of Cells
| Feature | Prokaryotic Cell | Eukaryotic Cell |
|---------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Nucleus** | No well-defined nuclear membrane; nucleoid region | Well-defined nucleus with nuclear membrane |
| **Membrane-bound organelles** | Absent | Present |
| **Size** | Generally smaller (0.1-5 micrometre) | Larger (5-100 micrometre) |
| **Examples** | Bacteria, blue-green algae (cyanobacteria) | Plant cells, animal cells, fungi, protists |
| **Ribosomes** | 70S type | 80S type |

### 1.3 Cell Membrane and Cell Wall
- **Cell membrane (Plasma membrane):** Selectively permeable membrane composed of lipids and proteins. Allows entry/exit of substances by diffusion and osmosis.
  - **Diffusion:** Movement of substances from higher to lower concentration (e.g., CO2, O2 exchange).
  - **Osmosis:** Movement of water from higher to lower water concentration through a semipermeable membrane.
    - **Hypotonic solution:** Cell swells (water enters).
    - **Isotonic solution:** No net movement.
    - **Hypertonic solution:** Cell shrinks (water leaves).
- **Cell wall (Plants):** Rigid outer covering made of cellulose; provides structural support; freely permeable.

### 1.4 Cell Organelles
| Organelle | Structure & Function |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **Endoplasmic Reticulum (ER)** | Network of membranes throughout the cytoplasm. **Rough ER:** Has ribosomes; involved in protein synthesis. **Smooth ER:** No ribosomes; involved in lipid synthesis and detoxification. |
| **Golgi Apparatus** | Stack of membrane-bound vesicles; packages, modifies, and transports proteins and lipids; forms lysosomes. |
| **Lysosomes** | Membrane-bound sacs containing digestive enzymes; "suicide bags" of the cell; digest foreign material and worn-out organelles. |
| **Mitochondria** | Double membrane-bound; "powerhouse of the cell"; site of cellular respiration; produces ATP; has its own DNA and ribosomes. |
| **Chloroplast** (Plant cells only) | Double membrane-bound; contains chlorophyll (green pigment); site of photosynthesis; "kitchen of the cell"; has its own DNA and ribosomes. |
| **Vacuoles** | Membrane-bound sacs; in plants: large central vacuole stores water, nutrients, waste; in animals: smaller and fewer. |
| **Nucleus** | Double membrane-bound (nuclear envelope with pores); contains genetic material (DNA/chromosomes); controls cell activities; has nucleolus (site of ribosome synthesis). |

### 1.5 Chromosomes
- Thread-like structures in the nucleus made of DNA and proteins.
- **Basic structure:** DNA wrapped around histone proteins; chromatin condenses into chromosomes during cell division.
- **Number:** Fixed for each species (Human: 46 or 23 pairs; Fruit fly: 8; Onion: 16).
- Genes are functional segments of DNA on chromosomes.

## 2. Key Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Cell** | The structural and functional unit of all living organisms. |
| **Prokaryote** | Organism whose cells lack a well-defined nucleus and membrane-bound organelles. |
| **Eukaryote** | Organism whose cells have a well-defined nucleus and membrane-bound organelles. |
| **Osmosis** | Movement of water through a selectively permeable membrane from higher to lower water concentration. |
| **Diffusion** | Movement of particles from higher to lower concentration. |
| **Plasmolysis** | Shrinkage of protoplasm due to exosmosis in a hypertonic solution. |
| **Endocytosis** | Ingestion of material by the cell through the plasma membrane. |
| **ATP** | Adenosine triphosphate - the energy currency of the cell. |

## 3. Board Exam Weightage & Question Pattern

Falls under **Unit II: Organization in the Living World (22 marks)**.

- **VSA (1 mark):** Name the organelle; define osmosis/diffusion; identify plasmolysis; which organelle is the powerhouse/kitchen/suicide bag.
- **SA (2-3 marks):** Differentiate prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells; diagram and label cell organelles; explain osmosis with example; differentiate plant and animal cells.
- **LA (5 marks):** Draw and label plant/animal cell; describe structure and function of cell organelles; explain types of solutions and their effects.
- **Diagram-based:** Plant cell, animal cell, prokaryotic cell, cell organelles.
