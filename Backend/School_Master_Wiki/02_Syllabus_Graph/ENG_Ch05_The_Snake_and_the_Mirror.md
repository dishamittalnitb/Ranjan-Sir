# Chapter 05: The Snake and the Mirror

## Theme & Summary

**Theme:** This humorous yet reflective story explores human vanity, the fragility of life, and how a moment of mortal danger can instantly transform one's perspective. It also touches upon the theme of fate and the unpredictability of life.

**Summary:** The narrator, a homeopathic doctor, narrates an incident from his early career. Living in a small, sparsely furnished rented room, he is admiring his looks in a mirror one hot summer night, thinking about his future wife (who must be a rich, fat doctor to match his slender frame). Suddenly, a full-blooded cobra lands on his shoulder and coils around his arm. Frozen with terror, the doctor sits motionless like a stone statue. The snake turns toward the mirror and seems fascinated by its own reflection. The doctor makes a silent escape. The next day, he shifts out, leaving behind most of his belongings. The story ends with a twist: the thief who later broke into the room left behind the doctor's dirty vest, showing the snake was still there.

## Character List

- **The Homeopathic Doctor (Narrator):** A young, vain, bachelor doctor who admires himself in the mirror; experiences a life-altering encounter with a cobra.
- **The Cobra:** A full-blooded, dangerous snake that coils around the doctor's arm but gets distracted by its reflection.

## Key Quotes

1. "I took a close look at my face in the mirror. I was a bachelor and a doctor."
2. "The sound was a familiar one. One could say that the rats and I shared the room."
3. "I sat there like a stone image in the flesh."
4. "It seemed as if God appreciated the joke and thought that the time had come to share it with the snake!"
