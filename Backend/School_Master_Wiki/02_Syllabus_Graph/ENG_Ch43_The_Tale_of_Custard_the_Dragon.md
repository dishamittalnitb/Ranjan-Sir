# Chapter 43: The Tale of Custard the Dragon

## Theme & Summary

**Theme:** This humorous ballad subverts expectations about bravery and cowardice. It suggests that true courage is revealed in moments of crisis, not in boasts. The poem playfully critiques those who brag about their bravery while mocking the one who appears timid — only for the "coward" to prove the bravest of all.

**Summary:** Belinda lives in a little white house with her four pets: a black kitten named Ink, a grey mouse named Blink, a yellow dog named Mustard, and a dragon named Custard. Ink, Blink, Mustard, and Belinda all boast of their bravery, while Custard — a dragon with "big sharp teeth" and "spikes on top of him" — is a coward who cries for a "nice safe cage." One day, a pirate with pistols in both hands and a cutlass between his teeth climbs through the window. Ink, Blink, and Mustard all flee in terror. But Custard, the supposedly cowardly dragon, gobbles up the pirate in one gulp. Afterward, everyone reverts to their boasting ways, and Custard again declares himself a coward — but the reader knows the truth.

## Literary Devices

- **Rhyme Scheme:** AABB (rhyming couplets) throughout, creating a light, sing-song ballad effect.
- **Alliteration:** "Belinda's little black kitten," "big sharp teeth," "pistol in his left hand, pistol in his right"
- **Repetition:** "And her little black kitten... And her little grey mouse... And her little yellow dog... And her realio, trulio, little pet dragon."
- **Humor:** Exaggerated descriptions, funny names (Ink, Blink, Mustard, Custard), and the absurd contrast between Custard's fearsome appearance and his cowardice.
- **Simile:** "Mouth like a fireplace," "chimney for a nose."
- **Onomatopoeia:** "growled," "clatter," "clank"
- **Refrain:** "But Custard cried for a nice safe cage."
- **Tone:** Playful, humorous, mock-epic.

## Key Quotes

1. "Belinda lived in a little white house, / With a little black kitten and a little grey mouse"
2. "Custard the dragon had big sharp teeth, / And spikes on top of him and scales underneath"
3. "Ink trickled down to the bottom of the household, / And little mouse Blink strategically mouseholed."
4. "But Custard gobbled him, every bit."
5. "Belinda embraced him, Mustard licked him, / No one mourned for his pirate victim."
