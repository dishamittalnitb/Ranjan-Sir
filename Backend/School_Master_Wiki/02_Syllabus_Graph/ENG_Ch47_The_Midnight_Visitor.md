# Chapter 47: The Midnight Visitor

## Theme & Summary

**Theme:** The story is a suspenseful spy thriller that demonstrates how quick thinking, presence of mind, and clever deception can outsmart a physically stronger opponent. It highlights that a sharp mind is a secret agent's greatest weapon, not physical strength.

**Summary:** Ausable, a secret agent unlike the typical spy (he is fat, slow, and speaks with an American accent despite claiming to be in Paris), receives a visitor in his hotel room — a writer named Fowler, who is disillusioned by the lack of "glamour" in actual espionage. Their conversation is interrupted by a knock: another secret agent, Max, enters with a gun, demanding a document about missiles that Ausable is supposed to receive. Ausable, unflustered, casually invents a story about his room having a balcony that people use to enter — a claim Max initially scoffs at. When there is a knock on the door (Ausable claims it's the police), Max decides to wait on the "balcony." He opens the window, steps out — and screams as he falls. There was no balcony. The knocker is just a waiter delivering drinks. Fowler, stunned, realizes that Ausable is a masterful secret agent after all.

## Character List

- **Ausable:** A brilliant secret agent who relies on wit, not muscles; fat, slow-moving, and deeply cunning.
- **Fowler:** A writer who expected glamour and excitement from espionage; serves as the reader's perspective.
- **Max:** An armed rival agent who is outsmarted by Ausable's fabricated balcony story.

## Key Quotes

1. "He was a fat, sloppy man and spoke French and German passably but never lost his American accent."
2. "There is a balcony outside this window. I use it. It is my second way out."
3. "The door opened, and it was only the waiter, with a tray."
4. "No, there never was a balcony."
